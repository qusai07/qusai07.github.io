AI Support Ticket Assistant

A bilingual support desk where an AI pipeline triages every ticket, retrieves policy and drafts a reply that cites its sources. Plain Python rules, not the model, decide what must go to a person, and reviewers approve, edit or escalate before anything is sent. The life of a ticket

  1. 1Ticket and its job are saved in one transaction; a worker picks it up
  2. 2Triage: category, priority, language and risk signals (LLM + rules)
  3. 3Rules escalate legal, safety, fraud and VIP tickets to the owning team
  4. 4Agent drafts a cited reply; guardrails check every number and ID against the evidence
  5. 5Review gate: send, or hand to a person with the reason shown
AI · Finance · Accounts payable

InvoiceFlow

AI invoice extraction with deterministic purchase-order matching. A vision model reads each PDF, scan or photo in Arabic or English. After that, C# makes every decision: arithmetic and UAE VAT checks, the supplier master, a fraud check for a changed IBAN, duplicate detection, and 2-way PO and 3-way goods-receipt matching within tolerances. Invoices that pass post to the ERP. Exceptions go to a reviewer, who can correct, override with a reason, or send the invoice for a four-eyes approval.

  • 40synthetic eval invoices
  • 9architecture decision records
  • 4-eyesapproval for high-value invoices
